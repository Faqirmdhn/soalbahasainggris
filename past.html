📖 Simple Past Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + V2 + O (-) S + did + not + V1 + O (?) Did + S + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian di masa lalu yang sudah selesai → I went to market yesterday
  • Kebiasaan di masa lalu → I played football every Sunday when I was a child
  • Fakta di masa lalu → He lived in London for 5 years

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I went to market yesterday
She ate pizza last night
They bought a new car
He visited his grandmother

🔄 Past Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + was/were + V-ing + O (-) S + was/were + not + V-ing + O (?) Was/Were + S + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu → I was watching TV when you called
  • Dua kejadian bersamaan di masa lalu → While I was cooking, she was sleeping
  • Suasana di masa lalu → It was raining heavily last night

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I was watching TV when you called
She was sleeping at 10 PM
They were playing football
It was raining heavily

✨ Past Perfect Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + had + V3 + O (-) S + had + not + V3 + O (?) Had + S + V3 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian yang selesai SEBELUM kejadian lain di masa lalu → I had finished my homework before dinner
  • Pengalaman di masa lalu → I had never seen such a beautiful place
  • Penyebab kejadian di masa lalu → He was tired because he had worked hard

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I had finished my homework before dinner
She had left when I arrived
They had eaten all the food
He had never been to Bali

⏳ Past Perfect Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + had + been + V-ing + O (-) S + had + not + been + V-ing + O (?) Had + S + been + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Durasi kejadian sebelum kejadian lain di masa lalu → I had been waiting for 2 hours when the bus came
  • Menekankan lamanya waktu → She had been studying for 5 years before moving
  • Penyebab dengan durasi → He was tired because he had been running

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I had been waiting for 2 hours
She had been studying for 5 years
They had been playing for an hour
It had been raining since morning
