Simple Past Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + V2 + O
(-) S + did + not + V1 + O
(?) Did + S + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lampau → I went to Bali last year
  • Kebiasaan di masa lalu (Past Habits) → I played soccer when I was a kid
  • Urutan aksi di masa lampau → He entered the room and sat down
Contoh Kalimat:
I went to market yesterday
She ate pizza last night
They bought a new car
He visited his grandmother

Past Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + was/were + V-ing + O
(-) S + was/were + not + V-ing + O
(?) Was/Were + S + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Sedang terjadi di waktu tertentu di masa lampau → I was studying at 8 PM
  • Kejadian berlangsung saat kejadian lain menyela → I was watching TV when you called
  • Dua aktivitas yang berjalan bersamaan → She was cooking while I was reading
Contoh Kalimat:
I was watching TV when you called
She was sleeping at 10 PM
They were playing football
It was raining heavily

Past Perfect Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + had + V3 + O
(-) S + had + not + V3 + O
(?) Had + S + V3 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian yang telah selesai sebelum kejadian masa lalu lainnya terjadi → She had left when I arrived
  • Pengalaman sebelum waktu tertentu di masa lalu → I had never seen that before 2020
  • Conditional Type 3 (penyesalan masa lalu) → If I had known, I would have told you
Contoh Kalimat:
I had finished my homework before dinner
She had left when I arrived
They had eaten all the food
I had never been to Bali before

Past Perfect Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + had + been + V-ing + O
(-) S + had + not + been + V-ing + O
(?) Had + S + been + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Durasi suatu aktivitas sebelum kejadian lampau lainnya → I had been waiting for 2 hours when the bus came
  • Menunjukkan penyebab kejadian di masa lampau → He was tired because he had been running
Contoh Kalimat:
I had been waiting for 2 hours when the bus came
She had been studying English for 5 years before moving
They had been playing for an hour when it rained
He had been working at the company for 10 years
