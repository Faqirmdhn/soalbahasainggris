✉️ Business Communication

Pelajari cara menulis email profesional, telepon bisnis, dan berpartisipasi dalam meeting

Email Writing

  • Dear Sir/Madam, - Salam pembuka formal Formal
  • I am writing to inquire about... - Menanyakan sesuatu Inquiry
  • Please find attached... - Mengirim lampiran Attachment
  • I look forward to hearing from you. - Menunggu balasan Closing
  • Sincerely, - Penutup formal Formal
  • Best regards, - Penutup semi-formal Semi-formal
📝 Contoh Email
Subject: Meeting Request

Dear Mr. Johnson,

I am writing to request a meeting to discuss the upcoming project. Please let me know your availability next week.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
Sarah

Telephoning

  • Good morning, this is [name] from [company]. - Memperkenalkan diri Opening
  • Could I speak to [name], please? - Minta bicara dengan seseorang Request
  • I'm calling about... - Menyebutkan tujuan telepon Purpose
  • Could you repeat that, please? - Minta diulang Clarification
  • I'll transfer you to [name]. - Mentransfer telepon Transfer
  • Thank you for calling. Goodbye. - Menutup telepon Closing

Meeting Phrases

  • Let's get started. - Membuka meeting Opening
  • The main agenda today is... - Menyebutkan agenda Agenda
  • Could you elaborate on that? - Minta penjelasan lebih lanjut Clarification
  • I see your point, but... - Setuju tapi ada catatan Polite Disagreement
  • Let's move on to the next point. - Pindah ke topik selanjutnya Transition
  • To sum up... - Menyimpulkan Closing
