🎤 Presentations

Pelajari cara membuka presentasi, menyusun struktur, dan menjawab pertanyaan dengan percaya diri

Opening a Presentation

  • Good morning, everyone. - Sapaan pembuka Greeting
  • Thank you for being here today. - Ucapan terima kasih Thanks
  • My name is [name] and I'm [position] at [company]. - Perkenalan diri Intro
  • The purpose of today's presentation is... - Menyampaikan tujuan Purpose
  • I've divided my presentation into [number] parts. - Menyampaikan struktur Structure
  • Feel free to interrupt if you have any questions. - Mempersilakan bertanya Invitation
🎯 Contoh Opening
"Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here today. My name is David and I'm the Marketing Director at TechCorp. The purpose of today's presentation is to introduce our new product line. I've divided my presentation into three parts. Feel free to interrupt if you have any questions."

Structuring

  • First, let me start with... - Memulai bagian pertama Transition
  • Moving on to the next point... - Pindah ke poin berikutnya Transition
  • This brings me to my next point... - Transisi ke poin berikutnya Transition
  • I'd like to highlight that... - Menekankan poin penting Emphasis
  • Let's take a look at the data... - Melihat data Data
  • To conclude... - Menyimpulkan Conclusion

Q&A Session

  • Are there any questions? - Membuka sesi tanya jawab Opening
  • That's a great question. - Merespon pertanyaan Response
  • Let me think about that for a moment. - Minta waktu berpikir Thinking
  • I'll get back to you on that. - Janji akan menjawab nanti Follow-up
  • Does that answer your question? - Memastikan puas Clarification
  • Thank you for your attention. - Menutup presentasi Closing
