👔 Job Interviews

Pelajari pertanyaan umum wawancara, metode STAR, dan tips menjawab dengan percaya diri

Common Questions

  • Tell me about yourself. - Ceritakan tentang dirimu. Opening
  • What are your greatest strengths? - Apa kelebihan terbesarmu? Strengths
  • What are your weaknesses? - Apa kelemahanmu? Weaknesses
  • Why do you want to work here? - Mengapa kamu ingin bekerja di sini? Motivation
  • Where do you see yourself in 5 years? - Di mana kamu melihat dirimu dalam 5 tahun? Future
  • Why should we hire you? - Mengapa kami harus merekrutmu? Closing
💡 Tips Menjawab
Tell me about yourself: "I have 5 years of experience in marketing. I specialize in digital strategy and I'm passionate about data-driven decisions. I'm currently looking for an opportunity to grow and lead a team."

Weaknesses: "I sometimes struggle with public speaking, but I've been taking courses to improve and now I feel more confident presenting to large groups."

STAR Method

  • Situation - SITUASI: Jelaskan konteksnya STAR
  • Task - TUGAS: Apa yang perlu dilakukan? STAR
  • Action - AKSI: Apa yang kamu lakukan? STAR
  • Result - HASIL: Apa hasilnya? STAR
⭐ Contoh Jawaban STAR
"Situation: Our team was falling behind on a major project deadline.
Task: I needed to reorganize our workflow and motivate the team.
Action: I implemented a new task management system and held daily stand-ups.
Result: We completed the project 2 days early and received great feedback."

Interview Tips

  • Research the company thoroughly. - Riset perusahaan dengan baik. Preparation
  • Practice common questions out loud. - Latihan menjawab pertanyaan dengan suara keras. Preparation
  • Prepare questions to ask the interviewer. - Siapkan pertanyaan untuk pewawancara. Questions
  • Dress professionally. - Berpakaian profesional. Appearance
  • Send a thank-you email after the interview. - Kirim email terima kasih setelah wawancara. Follow-up
