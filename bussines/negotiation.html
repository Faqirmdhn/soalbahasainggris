🤝 Negotiation

Pelajari cara tawar-menawar, setuju dan tidak setuju dengan sopan dalam negosiasi bisnis

Bargaining

  • Is there any room for negotiation? - Apakah ada ruang untuk negosiasi? Opening
  • We can offer a discount if... - Kami bisa beri diskon jika... Offer
  • What if we split the difference? - Bagaimana kalau kita bagi selisihnya? Compromise
  • I'm afraid that's not within our budget. - Sayangnya itu di luar anggaran kami. Reject
  • Could you meet us halfway? - Bisakah kamu bertemu di tengah jalan? Compromise
  • Let's find a win-win solution. - Mari cari solusi yang menguntungkan kedua pihak. Closing
💼 Contoh Negosiasi
"We really like your product, but the price is too high. Is there any room for negotiation? What if we split the difference? We can meet at $500 per unit."

Agreeing Politely

  • I completely agree with you. - Saya setuju sepenuhnya denganmu. Agree
  • That's a fair point. - Itu poin yang adil. Agree
  • You're absolutely right about that. - Kamu benar sekali tentang itu. Agree
  • I see what you mean. - Saya mengerti maksudmu. Agree
  • We're on the same page. - Kita sepaham. Agree

Disagreeing Politely

  • I understand your point, but... - Saya mengerti maksudmu, tapi... Disagree
  • I see it differently. - Saya melihatnya berbeda. Disagree
  • I'm not sure I agree with that. - Saya tidak yakin setuju dengan itu. Disagree
  • I respectfully disagree. - Saya dengan hormat tidak setuju. Disagree
  • Let me offer a different perspective. - Izinkan saya menawarkan perspektif berbeda. Alternative
