Simple Future Tense (Will)

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + V1 + O
(-) S + will + not (won't) + V1 + O
(?) Will + S + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Keputusan spontan saat berbicara → I will answer the phone
  • Prediksi tanpa bukti langsung → It will rain tomorrow
  • Janji (Promises) → I will call you later
  • Tawaran bantuan sukarela → I will help you with your bags
Contoh Kalimat:
I will call you tomorrow
She will help you
They will arrive at 8 PM
It will rain today

Be Going To Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + am/is/are + going to + V1 + O
(-) S + am/is/are + not + going to + V1 + O
(?) Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Rencana yang sudah diputuskan sebelumnya → I am going to study tonight
  • Prediksi dengan bukti nyata saat ini → Look at those clouds! It is going to rain
  • Niat atau cita-cita kuat → She is going to be a doctor
Contoh Kalimat:
I am going to buy a new car
Look at those clouds! It is going to rain
She is going to visit her grandmother
We are going to have a party

Future Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + be + V-ing + O
(-) S + will + not + be + V-ing + O
(?) Will + S + be + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa depan → At 8 PM tomorrow, I will be watching TV
  • Aktivitas yang terinterupsi di masa depan → I will be waiting when your flight lands
Contoh Kalimat:
I will be sleeping at 10 PM
She will be working tomorrow
They will be traveling next week
The sun will be rising at 6 AM

Future Perfect Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + have + V3 + O
(-) S + will + not + have + V3 + O
(?) Will + S + have + V3 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Aksi yang akan sudah selesai sebelum waktu tertentu di masa depan → By next year, I will have graduated
  • Menunjukkan pencapaian target masa depan → They will have built the bridge by December
Contoh Kalimat:
I will have finished by tomorrow
She will have left before you arrive
They will have arrived by noon
She will have graduated by next year

Future Perfect Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + have + been + V-ing + O
(-) S + will + not + have + been + V-ing + O
(?) Will + S + have + been + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Menekankan durasi suatu kegiatan hingga waktu tertentu di masa depan → By 2025, I will have been working here for 5 years
  • Sebab akibat dari suatu proses di masa depan → She will be tired because she will have been exercising for 2 hours
Contoh Kalimat:
I will have been waiting for 2 hours
She will have been teaching for 10 years
They will have been living here since 2010
He will have been running for an hour
