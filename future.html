🔮 Simple Future Tense (Will)

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + V1 + O (-) S + will + not + V1 + O (?) Will + S + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Keputusan spontan → I will answer the phone
  • Prediksi tanpa bukti → It will rain tomorrow
  • Janji → I will call you later
  • Tawaran bantuan → I will help you

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I will call you tomorrow
She will help you
They will arrive at 8 PM
It will rain today

🎯 Be Going To Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + am/is/are + going to + V1 + O (-) S + am/is/are + not + going to + V1 + O (?) Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V1 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Rencana yang sudah dibuat → I am going to study tonight
  • Prediksi dengan bukti → Look at those clouds! It is going to rain
  • Niat yang kuat → She is going to be a doctor

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I am going to buy a new car
She is going to visit her grandma
They are going to watch a movie
It is going to snow

🔄 Future Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + be + V-ing + O (-) S + will + not + be + V-ing + O (?) Will + S + be + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian sedang berlangsung di waktu tertentu di masa depan → At 8 PM, I will be watching TV
  • Kejadian yang pasti terjadi → The sun will be rising at 6 AM
  • Menanyakan rencana sopan → Will you be coming to the party?

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I will be sleeping at 10 PM
She will be working tomorrow
They will be traveling next week
He will be studying all night

✨ Future Perfect Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + have + V3 + O (-) S + will + not + have + V3 + O (?) Will + S + have + V3 + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Kejadian yang akan selesai SEBELUM waktu tertentu di masa depan → By 8 PM, I will have finished my homework
  • Target penyelesaian → She will have graduated by next year

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I will have finished by tomorrow
She will have left before you arrive
They will have arrived by noon
He will have eaten by then

⏳ Future Perfect Continuous Tense

📐 Pola Kalimat:

(+) S + will + have + been + V-ing + O (-) S + will + not + have + been + V-ing + O (?) Will + S + have + been + V-ing + O?

✨ Kapan Digunakan?

  • Durasi kejadian sampai waktu tertentu di masa depan → By next year, I will have been working here for 5 years
  • Menekankan lamanya waktu → She will have been studying for 3 hours by 9 PM

📝 Contoh Kalimat:

I will have been waiting for 2 hours
She will have been teaching for 10 years
They will have been living here since 2010
He will have been running for an hour
